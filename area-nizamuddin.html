Humayun’s Tomb

A grand red-sandstone mausoleum anchors a four-part garden threaded by water channels. Persian and Indian craftspeople helped establish a monumental Mughal language that later reached another celebrated expression at the Taj Mahal.

90 min visit

Nizamuddin

Isa Khan’s Tomb

Within the Humayun’s Tomb precinct, this earlier garden tomb commemorates the noble Isa Khan Niazi. Its octagonal shape offers a useful comparison with the much larger imperial mausoleum nearby.

30 min visit
