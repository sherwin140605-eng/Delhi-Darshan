Explore a neighbourhood, not just its landmarks

In Old Delhi, bazaars and places of worship are part of daily life. In Nizamuddin, monuments sit alongside a living neighbourhood and conservation work. Lodhi Colony brings public art into residential streets; murals and access can change.

Choose one anchor, keep an optional extension short, and allow an editorial 1–2 hours for a focused walk. Check the actual entrance and final journey, ask before photographing residents, keep doorways clear and follow worship-site customs. Festival descriptions explain cultural traditions; use the organiser-linked events guide for dated programmes.

Find more neighbourhood and street guides, or prepare your own observation walk. Historical context and changing practical details are separated throughout the guide.
