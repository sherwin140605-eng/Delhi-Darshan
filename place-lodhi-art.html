Lodhi Art District

DESTINATION OVERVIEW

About this destination

Public murals turn a neighbourhood walk into an open-air gallery. It is also a residential area: keep entrances clear and ask before photographing people.

Before you visit

Respect residents and staff, keep entrances clear, and ask before photographing people.

Photography guidance

Look for details without obstructing paths. Ask permission before photographing people or interiors.

Access & facilities

Public streets have variable kerbs and surfaces; no step-free route is verified.

Getting there

Jor Bagh Metro; continue by foot or a local connection. Use official journey planning for the final route.

Hours & tickets: Confirm current opening times, entry and booking arrangements directly.
No live availability or price feed is connected. Official visitor information
