Central Delhi

India Gate

Edwin Lutyens designed this imposing war memorial on New Delhi’s ceremonial axis. The arch and its surrounding public landscape offer a different perspective on the city from the enclosed courtyards of its older monuments.

45 min visit
India Gate · Delhi city contextMughal and Sur
Central Delhi

Purana Qila

Massive gateways and thick ramparts mark a city-building project associated with Humayun and continued under Sher Shah Suri. Look closely at the gateways, where pointed arches meet brackets and chhatris.

90 min visit
India Gate · Delhi city contextColonial
Central Delhi

Rashtrapati Bhavan

India’s presidential residence blends Edwin Lutyens’s classical composition with chhatris, jaalis and a dome influenced by Sanchi. Visits operate through distinct bookable circuits, so the main building, museum and gardens should be planned separately.

90 min visit
India Gate · Delhi city contextColonial
Central Delhi

Cathedral Church of the Redemption

Henry Medd’s church combines a restrained classical exterior with red sandstone accents. Close to Rashtrapati Bhavan, it is an active Church of North India cathedral rather than a museum.

30 min visit
