Connaught Place · Chole kulche

Chole kulche around Connaught Place

Soft kulcha and spiced peas or chickpeas make an easy lunch. Ask whether the stall serves matar or chole: the names are sometimes used loosely.

Get directions
DESTINATION OVERVIEW

Food and local context

An everyday lunch, full of character

What to order

Chole kulche. Ask what is freshly prepared; menu availability varies by vendor.

Dietary notes

Wheat; butter may be added. Tell the vendor about allergies and cross-contact needs. A preference label is not an allergen guarantee.

Before you visit

Respect residents and staff, keep entrances clear, and ask before photographing people.

Photography guidance

Look for details without obstructing paths. Ask permission before photographing people or interiors.

Access & facilities

Step-free access, accessible toilets and seating have not been confirmed. Ask the operator about your route and support needs.

Hours & tickets: Confirm current opening times, entry and booking arrangements directly.

Jantar Mantar

Jai Singh II’s monumental astronomical instruments turn geometry into architecture. Their sweeping curves and sharply angled surfaces were built for observations rather than decoration, making this a rewarding stop for curious students.

45 min visit
India Gate · Delhi city contextMedieval
Connaught Place

Agrasen ki Baoli

A descending flight of stone steps creates a striking pocket of historic water architecture close to central Delhi’s busy streets. The site’s origins attract many legends; these should not be confused with securely established dates.

30 min visit
India Gate · Delhi city contextLiving heritage

Gurudwara Bangla Sahib

This central Delhi gurdwara is connected with Guru Har Krishan’s stay in the city in 1664. Its active religious life makes it a place to approach with curiosity, quiet attention and respect for worshippers.

60 min visit
