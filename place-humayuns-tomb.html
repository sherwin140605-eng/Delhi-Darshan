Nizamuddin · Mughal

Humayun’s Tomb

A grand red-sandstone mausoleum anchors a four-part garden threaded by water channels. Persian and Indian craftspeople helped establish a monumental Mughal language that later reached another celebrated expression at the Taj Mahal.

About this destination

The wider World Heritage property includes earlier tombs such as Isa Khan’s.

Architecture & character

Mughal garden tomb. 1560s.

VISITOR NOTES · CHECKED 19 October 2026

Visitor information

Opening arrangements
Plan a daylight visit. Official tourism pages disagree about closing time, so confirm evening access before travelling.
Admission
Ticketed site. Delhi Tourism publishes differing fees on its old and new pages; use the ASI ticket portal to confirm the current category and inclusions.
Getting there
Official tourism pages identify JLN Stadium and Jangpura on different pages; choose your route using the live map. No exit is verified.

The adjoining World Heritage Site Museum is a separate attraction with its own hours, tickets and Monday closure; do not apply its schedule to the tomb.

Before you visit

Treat burial spaces respectfully and stay off garden beds.

Photography guidance

Use a garden axis to frame the central dome symmetrically.

Step-free entrances, accessible toilets and wheelchair availability have not been confirmed. Contact the operator about your specific route and support needs.

Isa Khan’s Tomb

Within the Humayun’s Tomb precinct, this earlier garden tomb commemorates the noble Isa Khan Niazi. Its octagonal shape offers a useful comparison with the much larger imperial mausoleum nearby.

30 min visit

Lodhi Road

Lodhi Garden

Garden paths weave between tombs, mosques and bridges from the Sayyid and Lodhi periods. The setting makes it easy to combine architectural observation with a relaxed walk among mature trees.

75 min visit

Central Delhi

Purana Qila

Massive gateways and thick ramparts mark a city-building project associated with Humayun and continued under Sher Shah Suri. Look closely at the gateways, where pointed arches meet brackets and chhatris.

90 min visit
