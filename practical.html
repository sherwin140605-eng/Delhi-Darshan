Getting around

Metro & buses

Use DMRC’s journey planner for actual routes, interchanges, operating times and fares. Allow for security checks and a final walk or ride. DTC provides official bus information. This guide does not calculate live fares.

Airport & railway stations

Confirm your terminal or station name: New Delhi, Old Delhi and Hazrat Nizamuddin are different stations. Match your airport connection to the correct terminal. Use official airport resources and IRCTC for railway booking.

Money & payments

UPI and cards are common at many businesses, but acceptance varies. Keep a modest cash backup. Check the recipient and amount before paying, and avoid scanning a QR code simply to “receive a refund”.

Travel precautions

Agree fares before a non-metered ride, use official ticket counters and verify claims that your hotel or attraction is closed. Keep valuables secure in crowded places and arrange a reliable return journey after dark.

Comfort & consideration

Weather & air

Hot weather, monsoon rain, winter fog and poor air quality can change a day’s plans. Check IMD and CPCB air-quality information. Leave outdoor plans flexible and choose indoor alternatives when needed.

Access & family visits

Ask operators about the actual entrance, ramps, lifts, walking surfaces, seating, toilets and wheelchair support. Historical stairs and crowded lanes can be difficult. Family-friendly suggestions describe interests, not verified child-care facilities.

Religious sites & photographs

Check dress and footwear rules. Cover your head where required, keep worship spaces quiet and ask before photographing people. Akshardham prohibits personal phones and cameras inside; the Lotus Temple restricts interior photography.

Pack lightly, travel thoughtfully

Bring comfortable shoes, water, sun protection, a charged phone, required identification and a small reusable bag. Avoid single-use waste, feeding wildlife or touching fragile stonework. Carry only what the site permits.
