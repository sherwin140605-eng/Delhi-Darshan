Hauz Khas · Delhi Sultanate

Hauz Khas

Medieval buildings overlook a historic reservoir beside today’s Hauz Khas Village. The mosque and madrasa remains connect the neighbourhood’s contemporary energy with the water infrastructure and learning spaces of Sultanate Delhi.

Mehrauli

Qutub Minar

The fluted sandstone minaret rises above a complex of mosques, tombs and gateways. Its carved bands and projecting balconies reward a slower look, while the surrounding ruins reveal several generations of Sultanate building.

90 min visit
India Gate · Delhi city contextMughal
Lodhi Road

Safdarjung Tomb

This late Mughal garden tomb was built for Safdarjung, the viceroy of Awadh. Its central mausoleum and enclosing garden carry forward the grand garden-tomb tradition on a more intimate scale.

60 min visit
