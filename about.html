ABOUT DELHI DARSHAN

About this Delhi travel guide

An independent editorial guide to Delhi’s heritage, food, culture and visitor information, for residents, students and travellers.

Our purpose

Delhi Darshan brings heritage, food, neighbourhoods and everyday culture into one accessible guide. It is an independent editorial project, not an official tourism authority or booking service. Its purpose is to make the city’s shared knowledge more approachable.

Built for first-time visitors and people who already call Delhi home, the guide pairs credited photographs with original illustrations and practical discovery tools. The community reading room welcomes original articles from Delhi visitors and residents. Sign in to submit your work, track review status and keep your contributions together.

Editorial & fact-checking policy

Historical descriptions draw on UNESCO, official operators, Delhi Tourism and other identified sources. The guide distinguishes literary associations and disputed claims from established facts. Practical content was checked on 14 October 2026; dates on source pages may be older.

Visit lengths and food budgets are editorial planning estimates. No rating, testimonial, live availability or public submission count is invented. Official links are supplied for changing prices, access rules, programmes and transport information. Reading guides are editorial introductions, not visitor testimonials. Events link to organiser-published schedules and leave the upcoming list after their end date.

Privacy & community publishing

Reading community articles does not require an account. When you create a Delhi Darshan account, we store your chosen name, email address and a protected password hash for authentication. Session cookies keep you signed in. Session records include normal connection information, such as your IP address and browser. If Google sign-in is enabled and you choose it, Google shares your account profile and verified email; we never receive your Google password. The account identifier links your submissions and reports to you; it is not displayed publicly. Your chosen pen name, article, area, category and publication date become public only after editorial approval. Pending articles, withdrawn articles, editorial notes and reports are visible only to the author where applicable and the editor. Community articles and reports are saved on the hosted service, so submitted work can be accessed across devices. The account email is not stored in article records or displayed on articles.

Withdraw an article from My articles to remove it from the public reading room. Contact the editor for a deletion request or a privacy concern. Saved drafts remain private to this browser until you submit them. Social links open Instagram and Facebook only when you click; social embeds and tracking widgets are not loaded.

Bookmarks, recent views, game progress, food trails, saved itineraries and article drafts can be kept in your browser’s local storage. They do not synchronise between devices and can disappear when browser data is cleared. Do not store sensitive information in a draft. Download any work you want to retain.

This downloaded edition embeds its images, fonts and scripts and makes no automatic map tile requests. Its map shows approximate pins without a street basemap. The live online guide can load OpenStreetMap tiles. Google Maps search and directions links open Google’s service. If an embedded Google map is enabled, it loads only when you choose to open it; Google then receives your search query and normal browser connection information. External links take you to other services with their own policies. Photography, icons, fonts and site scripts are served with the site; no analytics tracker is added by this project. The hosting platform may operate its own service logs and access controls.

Terms of use

This guide provides editorial information and planning tools, not transport, bookings or guaranteed access. Check official arrangements before travelling. Third-party images and fonts remain governed by their credited licences; these terms do not override those licences. Site content may contain errors and changes can occur after checking.

Accessibility statement

The interface uses semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, labelled controls, reduced-motion support, native dialogs, text alternatives and responsive layouts. The map’s place list offers an alternative to interacting with markers. Individual venue accessibility is not implied by the website’s accessibility features.

Interactive features require JavaScript. Where third-party map tiles are unavailable, place descriptions and external map links remain usable. No claim of independently audited WCAG conformance is made.

Community guidelines

Share original, respectful work, credit photographs and obtain permission where needed. Do not include private contact information, identifying details about children, harassment or unverified accusations. Submitted articles are reviewed before publication. Create a Delhi Darshan account or sign in, then choose a public pen name. Share text you own or have permission to use; credit quotations and research sources. The editor may request changes or remove a contribution. Changes to a published article return it to review. Readers can report concerns on an article or contact the editor. Contributors retain ownership and permit Delhi Darshan to display accepted work; withdrawing it removes it from public view. This edition accepts text articles, and does not accept public photo uploads.
