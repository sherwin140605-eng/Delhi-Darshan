One city, many celebration tables

Delhi Darshan editorialPublished 2026-10-141 min readEditorial reading guide

A box of sweets can be a greeting, a gift or a way of marking a milestone. Delhi’s communities bring distinct food traditions to festivals and family occasions.

Be curious without assuming everyone follows the same customs. Ask hosts about the meaning of a dish and respect dietary choices, fasting and religious practices.

Discover how local food traditions meet in everyday streets. The culture guide links to starting points for further exploration.
