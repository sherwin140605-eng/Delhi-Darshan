Delhi Metro travel guide for first-time visitors

Delhi Darshan editorialPublished 2026-10-223 min readEditorial reading guide

Start with the entrance, not just the landmark

Find the attraction’s actual visitor entrance on the Delhi map, then use DMRC’s official Journey Planner to choose your starting and destination stations. A station with a familiar place name is not always the most useful exit for that entrance. Check the final walking or road connection separately. This guide links to the operator rather than calculating live Metro fares or running times.

Check the whole journey before buying a ticket

Read the line, direction of travel, interchange and destination on the journey result. Allow time for entering the station, security and changing platforms. For the return trip, use DMRC’s first/last-train and station information for the specific journey; there is no single departure time that describes the whole network. Leave extra time for a connection to a railway station or an airport terminal.

Tickets and QR entry

DMRC supports digital QR tickets. Use its official app-based QR ticket information to find current channels and instructions. Confirm the journey and ticket conditions before paying. Keep your ticket or phone available for both entry and exit, follow the gate instructions, and ask station staff if a gate will not read it. Do not assume that a screenshot, expired code or another person’s ticket will work.

Match a Metro trip to one sightseeing area

Our Metro mini-trips offer compact ideas around Old Delhi, central Delhi and the Lodhi area. Treat their timings and budgets as editable allowances. Check their station and last-mile leg against the day’s DMRC information before going. A map pin identifies a place in the guide; it does not verify the entrance is open or that the route has step-free access.

Access and travelling with children

DMRC’s published accessibility material describes lifts, ramps, tactile features and staff assistance. Check the specific station, entrance and interchange for your needs, and allow time to find a working lift. The street between the station and attraction can still have uneven paving or difficult crossings. With children, agree a meeting point, stay clear of platform edges and let passengers leave before boarding. Ask venue operators separately about toilets, seating and stroller or wheelchair arrangements.

Airport and railway connections

Confirm the precise airport terminal or railway station before choosing a route. New Delhi, Old Delhi and Hazrat Nizamuddin are different railway stations. Include the time from the Metro exit to the correct railway entrance or airport check-in area, plus the carrier’s required arrival buffer. Use operator information for luggage rules and any unusual service arrangements.

A useful pre-trip checklist

Save the official route, check the final entrance, confirm the last practical return connection, and keep enough battery and a payment backup. When the plan changes, recheck the journey instead of relying on an old fare or screenshot. Pair this guide with Delhi in one day or the budget-day guide to keep travel and sightseeing in balance.

Sources and practical guidance reviewed 2026-10-22. Check operator information again before travel; timings are editorial estimates.
